# BOARD DIRECTIVE 143 — STRATEGIC CAPITAL ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE VALUE | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Capital exists in many forms. Money is only one of them.

## CAPITAL TYPES
Knowledge, Trust, Brand, Customer, Marketplace, Automation, Data, AI, Execution, Time Capital.

## MEASURE
Growth, Quality, Risk, Compounding, Return.

## RULE
Protect strategic capital before financial capital.

## FINAL DECLARATION
The richest companies own invisible assets.
