# BOARD DIRECTIVE 128 — ORGANIZATIONAL RESILIENCE ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** RISK MANAGEMENT | **OWNER:** Enterprise Health Engine

## MISSION
Continuously evaluate the resilience of the entire enterprise. Identify vulnerabilities before they become failures.

## MONITOR
Revenue Concentration, Customer Dependency, Supplier Dependency, Technical Risks, Knowledge Risks, Operational Risks, AI Risks, Marketplace Risks, Legal Risks, Security Risks.

## FOR EVERY RISK — DISPLAY
Likelihood, Impact, Exposure, Trend, Mitigation Plan, Recovery Time, Owner, Priority.

## SELF HEALING
Whenever possible, recommend preventive actions before intervention becomes necessary.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A resilient company survives uncertainty. An intelligent company anticipates it.
