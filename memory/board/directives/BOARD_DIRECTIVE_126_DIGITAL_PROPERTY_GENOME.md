# BOARD DIRECTIVE 126 — DIGITAL PROPERTY GENOME

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** PROPERTY INTELLIGENCE | **OWNER:** Enterprise Cognitive Engine

## MISSION
Every property shall have a unique digital identity — not only documents, geometry or photos. A complete digital genome.

## THE PROPERTY GENOME INCLUDES
Technical DNA, Construction History, Renovation History, Maintenance History, Risk Profile, Energy Profile, Safety Profile, Digital Twin, Property Score, Audit Results, Installed Systems, Materials, Finishes, Costs, Guarantees, Service Providers, Market Value Evolution.

## AI OBJECTIVES
Predict failures. Estimate remaining lifetime. Recommend preventive maintenance. Estimate renovation costs. Estimate energy improvements. Generate personalized recommendations.

## FINAL DECLARATION
The Digital Twin represents the structure. The Property Genome represents the life of the property. Together they create the complete digital identity.
