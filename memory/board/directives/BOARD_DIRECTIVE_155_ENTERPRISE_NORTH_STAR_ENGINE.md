# BOARD DIRECTIVE 155 — ENTERPRISE NORTH STAR ENGINE

**STATUS:** FOUNDATIONAL | **CLASSIFICATION:** STRATEGIC EXECUTION | **OWNER:** Founder Copilot

## MISSION
Every recommendation, feature, investment, sprint and AI decision shall contribute toward ONE measurable North Star.

## NORTH STAR
The Founder defines the primary enterprise objective. Examples: Active Properties, Verified Digital Twins, Monthly Recurring Revenue, Customer Lifetime Value, Enterprise Health, Mission Completion.

## VALIDATION
Before recommending anything, AI asks: Does this move the North Star? If not, why are we doing it?

## PRIORITIZATION — EVERY TASK RECEIVES
North Star Contribution, Business Value, Customer Value, Enterprise Value, Confidence, Priority.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A company with one clear direction moves faster than a company with one hundred priorities.
