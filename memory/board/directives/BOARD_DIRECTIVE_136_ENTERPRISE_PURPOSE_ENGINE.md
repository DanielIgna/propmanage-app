# BOARD DIRECTIVE 136 — ENTERPRISE PURPOSE ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** STRATEGIC FOUNDATION | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Every recommendation, feature, workflow, investment evaluated against the purpose of PropManage. Purpose > activity. Direction > speed.

## PURPOSE VALIDATION — BEFORE ANY INITIATIVE
Does it improve customer value? Strengthen the ecosystem? Simplify the platform? Increase trust? Increase enterprise value? Align with long-term strategy?

## PURPOSE SCORE — EVERY PROJECT
Strategic Alignment, Customer Alignment, Enterprise Value, Innovation Value, Knowledge Value, Trust Value, Long-Term Sustainability.

## REJECTION RULE
Reject initiatives that create complexity without measurable long-term value.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A company without purpose becomes busy. A company with purpose becomes exceptional.
