# BOARD DIRECTIVE 119 — MARKET EXPANSION ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** MARKET GROWTH | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Every unmet customer request represents an opportunity. PropManage shall transform market demand into market expansion. The platform shall never wait for specialists — it shall identify, measure, prioritize and recruit where demand already exists.

## DEMAND TRIGGERS — MONITOR CONTINUOUSLY
Open Gap Records, Cities without specialists, Categories without specialists, Waiting customers, Estimated lost revenue, Repeated customer requests, Average waiting time.

## EXPANSION SCORE — PER CITY/CATEGORY
Demand Score, Supply Score, Revenue Potential, Urgency, Growth Trend, Recruitment Priority.

## AUTOMATIC ACTIONS — WHEN THRESHOLDS EXCEEDED
Create public recruitment page. Generate SEO landing page. Generate recruitment content. Notify Founder. Notify Growth Office. Notify Marketplace Office. Create recruitment campaign.

## PUBLIC LANDING PAGE — AUTO-PUBLISH
"We are looking for verified specialists." Display: City, Category, Estimated monthly demand, Benefits of joining, Application Form, Referral Bonus (optional).

## FOUNDER DASHBOARD
Top Expansion Cities, Top Missing Categories, Estimated Lost Revenue, Specialists Needed, Applications Received, Time to Fill.

## LEARNING — TRACK
Application sources, Conversion rate, Hiring success, Customer waiting time, Revenue recovered.

## RULE
Expansion shall be driven by verified customer demand. Never recruit based on assumptions. Recruit where customers are already waiting.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Every Gap is an opportunity. Every opportunity becomes expansion. Every expansion increases customer value.
