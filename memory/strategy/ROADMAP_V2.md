Capabilities: Revenue Forecast, Churn Prediction, Specialist Demand Prediction, Risk Forecast, Marketplace Forecast, Maintenance Prediction.

Objective: optimize continuously. Every night: analyze code/UX/database/infrastructure/performance/feedback → generate proposals → execute safe improvements automatically.

Objective: marketplace grows by itself. AI continuously: recruits specialists, detects demand, identifies underserved areas, creates landing pages, launches recruitment campaigns, proposes partnerships (→ D119).
KPI: marketplace expands with minimal manual management.

## PHASE G — ENTERPRISE INTELLIGENCE ✅ (implementat: CEO Briefing D152)
Objective: Founder receives ONE executive briefing per day: Enterprise Score, Top Risks, Top Opportunities, Recommended Actions, Autonomous Actions Completed, Decisions Awaiting Approval. No dashboards — only decisions.

## PHASE H — FOUNDER EVOLUTION
Objective: Founder transitions from operator to strategist. Target time: 60% Strategy, 20% Partnerships, 10% Product Vision, 10% Executive Decisions, 0% repetitive operational work.

## THE FINAL SUCCESS METRIC
PropManage is successful when: customers solve problems faster; specialists receive better-qualified work; properties accumulate useful knowledge; the enterprise becomes simpler, not more complex; AI demonstrably saves time and reduces risk; every release improves Enterprise Health with evidence; the company operates effectively even if the Founder is unavailable for an extended period.
