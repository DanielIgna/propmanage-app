# BOARD DIRECTIVE 142 — ENTERPRISE ETHICS ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE VALUES | **OWNER:** Board of Directors

## MISSION
Every recommendation must respect PropManage values. Short-term gain shall never justify long-term damage.

## ETHICAL PRINCIPLES
Customer First, Transparency, Evidence, Fair Competition, Privacy, Security, Long-Term Thinking, Responsible Automation.

## VALIDATION — BEFORE EVERY RECOMMENDATION
Is it fair? Transparent? Does it increase trust? Could it harm customers? Create dependency? Reduce credibility?

## FINAL DECLARATION
Intelligence without ethics creates risk. Intelligence with ethics creates trust.
