# BOARD DIRECTIVE 140 — LEGACY ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE PHILOSOPHY | **OWNER:** Board of Directors

## MISSION
Build a company that creates value for decades. Not quarters.

## EVERY DECISION — ASK
Will this still matter in 10 years? Will customers benefit? Will knowledge increase? Will trust increase? Will future teams thank us?

## LEGACY SCORE
Long-Term Value, Knowledge Contribution, Customer Impact, Enterprise Impact, Sustainability, Strategic Importance.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Software, features, prices can be copied. Culture, knowledge, trust, legacy cannot. Build all three.
