# BOARD DIRECTIVE 152 — CEO BRIEFING ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** EXECUTIVE AI | **OWNER:** Founder Copilot

## MISSION
Every day the Founder receives ONE executive briefing. Not dashboards. Not reports. Not hundreds of metrics. One page.

## EVERY BRIEFING
Enterprise Status, Overall Health, Mission Progress, Revenue, Customer Activity, Marketplace, Operations, Growth, Knowledge, AI Learning.

## MOST IMPORTANT SECTION
"If you only do ONE thing today, do this." — with Why, Expected ROI, Expected ROT, Expected Health Impact, Confidence.

## TOP RISKS
Maximum 5.

## TOP OPPORTUNITIES
Maximum 5.

## FOUNDER FOCUS
What should be ignored today? What should be delegated? What absolutely requires Founder attention?

## NOISE FILTER
Hide everything that does not influence today's decisions.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A CEO does not need more information. A CEO needs better priorities.
