# BOARD DIRECTIVE 122 — ENTERPRISE HEALTH ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** EXECUTIVE MONITORING | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## FOUNDER IDEA (verbatim intent)
Modul Admin "Enterprise Health" — nu un dashboard clasic, ci un "scor de sănătate" al întregii companii, ex:
Enterprise Health: 87/100 — Product 92, UX 88, Operations 90, Growth 76, Marketplace 81, Customer Trust 94, Knowledge 91, Revenue 63, Automation 85, Technical Debt 89, AI Learning 93.
Când un scor scade sub prag (ex. 80), AI generează automat: explicația cauzei, impactul asupra afacerii, primele 3 acțiuni recomandate, estimarea efectului dacă sunt implementate.

## MISSION
Founder shall never inspect hundreds of reports. The health of the entire company visible in one place. Enterprise Health = operational heartbeat: continuously measures, analyzes, predicts and explains the health of every major business system.

## ENTERPRISE HEALTH SCORE
One Overall Enterprise Health Score (0–100), calculated from all strategic domains. Every score must be evidence-based.

## HEALTH DOMAINS
Product, UX, Operations, Marketplace, Revenue, Customer Trust, Knowledge Growth, Growth Engine, Mission 100, AI Performance, Automation, Security, Performance, Technical Debt, Documentation, Enterprise Value, Franchise Readiness.

## FOR EACH DOMAIN — DISPLAY
Current Score, Previous Score, 30-Day Trend, Confidence Level, Risk Level, Business Impact, Top 5 Findings, Top 5 Improvements, ROT Score, Owner.

## COLOR SYSTEM
95–100 World Class | 90–94 Excellent | 80–89 Healthy | 70–79 Needs Attention | 60–69 At Risk | <60 Critical.

## HEALTH EXPLANATION — EVERY SCORE ANSWERS
Why is the score what it is? What improved? What declined? What caused the decline? Recommended action? What KPI will improve?

## EXECUTIVE RADAR — AUTO-DETECT
Rapid KPI decline, Revenue anomalies, UX degradation, Marketplace imbalance, Customer frustration, Support overload, Lead quality decline, Knowledge stagnation, Operational bottlenecks, Growth slowdown, Technical debt increase.

## PREDICTIVE ENGINE
Future Revenue, Mission 100 completion, Lead growth, Marketplace balance, Customer retention, Expansion opportunities, Operational risk.

## FOUNDER DASHBOARD
Overall Enterprise Health + Top 5: Risks, Opportunities, Decisions, Quick Wins, AI Recommendations, Founder Priorities.

## ACTION CENTER — EVERY RECOMMENDATION INCLUDES
Evidence, Expected ROI, Estimated Time, Estimated Cost, ROT Score, Dependencies, Affected Modules, Responsible Owner, Success KPI.

## LEARNING
Track approved recommendations, measure actual impact, adjust future recommendations, increase confidence in successful patterns, discard ineffective ones.

## RULE
Scores generated only from measurable evidence, never assumptions.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Purpose is helping the Founder make better decisions every day. A healthy company compounds value. A measurable company improves continuously. An intelligent company predicts problems before they become crises.
