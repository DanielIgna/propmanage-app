# ENTERPRISE EVOLUTION CONTRACT — VERSION 1.0 (ACTIVE)

## ARTICLE 1 — THE MISSION
PropManage shall continuously evolve. Evolution is mandatory. Random change is forbidden.

## ARTICLE 2 — THE RULE OF EVIDENCE
No feature, AI, automation, workflow or architecture shall exist without measurable value. Evidence always wins. Opinions never override evidence.

## ARTICLE 3 — THE RULE OF SIMPLICITY
Every release must become simpler, clearer, faster, more maintainable or more valuable. If complexity increases, value must increase more — otherwise reject the implementation.

## ARTICLE 4 — THE RULE OF LEARNING
Every success must become knowledge. Every failure must become knowledge. Nothing important shall ever be forgotten.

## ARTICLE 5 — THE RULE OF AUTONOMY
AI continuously assumes repetitive, measurable, low-risk work. Human intelligence focuses on strategy, innovation, relationships and irreversible decisions.

## ARTICLE 6 — THE RULE OF TRUST
Trust is the primary enterprise asset. Revenue follows trust. Never sacrifice trust for short-term gain.

## ARTICLE 7 — THE RULE OF VALUE
Everything created must improve at least one of: Customer Value, Enterprise Health, Knowledge, Revenue, Trust, Operational Excellence, Long-Term Sustainability. Otherwise, do not build it.

## ARTICLE 8 — THE RULE OF REMOVAL
Removing obsolete code/AI/features/processes is progress. Simplification is innovation.

## ARTICLE 9 — THE RULE OF EVOLUTION
Every version shall be measurably better than the previous — measured by evidence, not opinions.

## ARTICLE 10 — THE LEGACY
The purpose of PropManage is not to become the biggest platform. It is to become the most trusted, the most intelligent, the most transparent and the most continuously improving Property Operating System.
