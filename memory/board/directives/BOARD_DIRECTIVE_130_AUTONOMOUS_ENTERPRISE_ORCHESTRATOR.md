# BOARD DIRECTIVE 130 — AUTONOMOUS ENTERPRISE ORCHESTRATOR

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE ORCHESTRATION | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Coordinate every intelligent system inside PropManage. Every engine, audit, recommendation, workflow operates as one coherent ecosystem.

## ORCHESTRATED SYSTEMS
Enterprise Health, Executive Decision Center, Mission 100, Operations Center, War Room, Knowledge Graph, Marketplace, Growth Engine, Trust Engine, Revenue Intelligence, AI Audit, UX Intelligence, Digital Twin, Property Genome, Innovation Engine.

## RESPONSIBILITIES
Resolve conflicts, Prioritize execution, Balance resources, Prevent duplicated work, Synchronize objectives, Measure enterprise progress, Maintain strategic alignment.

## GOVERNANCE
No autonomous action may contradict Board Directives. No optimization may reduce customer value for short-term gains.

## FINAL DECLARATION
PropManage shall operate as one intelligent organism, not as disconnected modules. Every system shall strengthen every other system.
