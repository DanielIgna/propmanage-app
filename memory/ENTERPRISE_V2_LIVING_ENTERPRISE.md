# ENTERPRISE CONSTITUTION v2 — THE LAWS OF EVOLUTION
Nu sunt reguli tehnice. Sunt legi.

**BOARD LAW 001 — VALUE BEFORE FEATURES:** No feature shall exist unless it creates measurable value. Features are temporary. Value is permanent.
**BOARD LAW 002 — SIMPLICITY IS A FEATURE:** Every release must become simpler than the previous. Complexity must always justify itself.
**BOARD LAW 003 — EVERYTHING HAS AN OWNER:** Every capability, AI, process, KPI, document, customer journey, integration has ONE accountable owner.
**BOARD LAW 004 — EVERYTHING IS MEASURED:** If it cannot be measured, it cannot be improved. If it creates no value, it should disappear.
**BOARD LAW 005 — ENTERPRISE MEMORY IS SACRED:** Never lose knowledge. Every failure, success, experiment, customer, decision becomes enterprise memory.
**BOARD LAW 006 — REMOVE MORE THAN YOU ADD:** Every quarter remove more complexity than you introduce. Simplicity compounds.
**BOARD LAW 007 — AI EXISTS TO REMOVE WORK:** AI does not exist to impress people. It exists to eliminate repetitive work and improve decisions.
**BOARD LAW 008 — CUSTOMERS DEFINE SUCCESS:** Internal opinions never override customer evidence. Customers decide whether value exists.
**BOARD LAW 009 — AUTONOMY REQUIRES RESPONSIBILITY:** Higher autonomy requires higher observability, testing, accountability, explainability.
**BOARD LAW 010 — THE ENTERPRISE MUST OUTLIVE ITS CREATORS:** More intelligent every year — independent of technology, people, AI models or founders.

## CONSTITUTION CHECK
Before merging any code, deploying, creating AI, creating workflow or changing architecture → run Constitution Check against these laws.
