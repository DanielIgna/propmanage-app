# BOARD DIRECTIVE 154 — ENTERPRISE DIGITAL DNA

**STATUS:** FOUNDATIONAL | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE IDENTITY | **OWNER:** Enterprise Cognitive Engine

## MISSION
Everything inside PropManage shall have a unique identity. Nothing shall exist without context, relationships, history, ownership and purpose.

## EVERY ENTITY SHALL HAVE
Unique ID, Lifecycle, Owner, Status, Relationships, History, Evidence, Knowledge Links, Business Value, Health Score, Trust Score.

## SUPPORTED ENTITIES
Users, Properties, Digital Twins, Audits, Projects, Leads, Customers, Specialists, Companies, Associations, Documents, Invoices, Knowledge, AI Recommendations, Tasks, Goals.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Identity creates traceability. Traceability creates intelligence.
