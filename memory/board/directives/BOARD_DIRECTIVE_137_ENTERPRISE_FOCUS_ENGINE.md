# BOARD DIRECTIVE 137 — ENTERPRISE FOCUS ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** EXECUTION | **OWNER:** Founder Copilot

## MISSION
Protect enterprise focus. The biggest threat to growth is not lack of ideas — it is lack of focus.

## DETECT
Too many parallel projects, Feature overload, Context switching, Unfinished initiatives, Low-impact work, Duplicate development.

## AI RESPONSIBILITIES
Recommend stopping / postponing / simplifying work. Recommend finishing before starting.

## FOCUS INDEX
Execution Focus, Founder Focus, Team Focus, Product Focus, Strategic Focus.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Focus compounds execution. Execution compounds value.
