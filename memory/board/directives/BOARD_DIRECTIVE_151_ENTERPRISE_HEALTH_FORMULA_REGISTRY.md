# BOARD DIRECTIVE 151 — ENTERPRISE HEALTH FORMULA REGISTRY
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE GOVERNANCE | **OWNER:** Enterprise Health Engine

## MISSION
Every Enterprise Health Score shall be transparent, traceable, versioned, configurable and fully explainable. No score without a documented formula.

## FORMULA REGISTRY
Centralized. Every Enterprise Health metric registered: Overall, Product, UX, Operations, Marketplace, Revenue, Trust, Growth, Knowledge, Automation, AI, Technical Debt, Mission Health.

## EVERY FORMULA CONTAINS
Unique ID, Name, Description, Business Objective, Calculation Formula, Input Metrics, Data Sources, Weights, Normalization Method, Score Range, Target Value, Warning Threshold, Critical Threshold, Version, Author, Created/Modified Date, Status.

## EXPLAIN SCORE — EVERY SCORE PROVIDES
Current Value, Calculation Steps, Data Used, Weight of Each Metric, Positive Contributors, Negative Contributors, Confidence Level, Historical Comparison.

## VERSIONING — EVERY UPDATE CREATES
New Version, Change Log, Reason, Expected Impact, Migration Notes, Backward Compatibility.

## ADMINISTRATION — FOUNDER MAY
Create, Edit, Disable, Archive, Duplicate, Compare Versions, Rollback Previous Version.

## VALIDATION — BEFORE ACTIVATION
Formula integrity, Data availability, Division by zero, Missing values, Weight consistency, Threshold consistency, Performance impact.

## AUDIT — PERMANENT LOG
Who modified, When, Why, Previous Version, New Version, Business Impact.

## RULES
No hardcoded business formulas. No hidden calculations. No undocumented weights. No score without explanation. Every formula reusable.

## API
Expose all formulas through internal APIs for: Enterprise Health, Executive Dashboard, Founder Copilot, Decision Center, Reports, Analytics, Future AI Engines.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Enterprise Health is trusted because every score can be explained, verified, versioned and continuously improved. Transparency creates confidence. Confidence creates better decisions.
