# BOARD DIRECTIVE 120 — ENTERPRISE INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** ENTERPRISE AI | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Every action inside PropManage generates intelligence. The platform continuously analyzes itself, its users, specialists, customers, marketplace and business performance. Objective: continuously improve the entire ecosystem.

## INTELLIGENCE DOMAINS
Product, UX, Marketplace, Customer, Specialist, Revenue, Knowledge, Operations, Growth, Trust Intelligence.

## CONTINUOUS AUDITS
Architecture, User Experience, Performance, Operations, Customer Journey, Marketplace Balance, Knowledge Quality, SEO, Content, Security, Enterprise Value.

## FOR EVERY AUDIT — PRODUCE
Current Score, Previous Score, Trend, Risk Level, Confidence Level, Business Impact, Top Problems, Top Opportunities, Recommended Actions, ROT Score.

## EXECUTIVE RADAR — DETECT AUTOMATICALLY
Pages with falling conversion, Growing demand, Growing frustration, Dead features, Unused buttons, Complex navigation, Slow workflows, Specialist shortages, Customer bottlenecks, Revenue leakage, Knowledge gaps.

## AI RECOMMENDATIONS
Never generic. Must include: Evidence, Expected KPI impact, Estimated implementation effort, Estimated ROI, Priority.

## SELF EVOLUTION
Track every accepted recommendation. Successful → increase confidence. Unsuccessful → store lesson, adjust future recommendations.

## EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD
Overall Enterprise Score, Top 10 Risks / Opportunities / UX Problems / Marketplace Gaps / Revenue Opportunities / Automation Opportunities / Quick Wins.

## RULE
Do not optimize randomly. Optimize only where evidence exists.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Purpose is not reporting — it is better decisions. Every audit must increase clarity. Every recommendation must increase enterprise value.
