# BOARD DIRECTIVE 127 — ENTERPRISE SIMULATION ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** STRATEGIC INTELLIGENCE | **OWNER:** Enterprise Cognitive Engine

## MISSION
Before any major decision is implemented — simulate the future. Reduce uncertainty by evaluating multiple possible outcomes.

## SIMULATION TYPES
Revenue, Marketplace Growth, Mission 100 Progress, Pricing Changes, Marketing Campaigns, Hiring Specialists, Expansion to New Cities, New Services, Subscription Changes, AI Automation.

## FOR EVERY SIMULATION — ESTIMATE
Best Case, Expected Case, Worst Case, Probability, Revenue Impact, Cost Impact, Customer Impact, Operational Impact, Risk Score, Confidence Score, ROT Score.

## AI RESPONSIBILITY
Never recommend strategic changes without first simulating their consequences.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A company that simulates the future makes better decisions in the present.
