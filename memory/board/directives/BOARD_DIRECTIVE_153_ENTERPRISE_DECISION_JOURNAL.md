# BOARD DIRECTIVE 153 — ENTERPRISE DECISION JOURNAL

**STATUS:** FOUNDATIONAL | **CLASSIFICATION:** KNOWLEDGE GOVERNANCE | **OWNER:** Executive Decision Center

## MISSION
Every important decision becomes permanent enterprise knowledge. No strategic decision shall ever be forgotten.

## FOR EVERY DECISION — STORE
Decision, Decision Type, Problem, Context, Evidence, Alternatives Considered, Chosen Option, Reason, Expected Outcome, Expected ROI, Expected ROT, Decision Owner, Approval Date, Related Projects, Related Board Directives, Related Standards, Related Playbooks.

## AFTER EXECUTION — MEASURE
Actual Result, Actual ROI, Actual ROT, Lessons Learned, Unexpected Outcomes, Knowledge Created.

## AI CAPABILITIES
Find similar decisions. Prevent repeated mistakes. Recommend historical solutions. Measure decision quality over time.

## FINAL DECLARATION
Companies improve when decisions become knowledge.
