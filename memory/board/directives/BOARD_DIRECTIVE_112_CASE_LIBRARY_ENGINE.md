# BOARD DIRECTIVE 112 — CASE LIBRARY ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** KNOWLEDGE & MARKETING | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Every completed project becomes a reusable company asset. No audit, Digital Twin, inspection, intervention, or customer success story shall disappear after completion.

## FOR EVERY PROJECT CAPTURE
Project Type, City, Property Type, Property Age, Surface Area, Requested Service, Problems Found, Root Causes, Recommendations, Estimated Savings, Estimated Risks Avoided, Photos (Before/After), Documents Generated, Time Required, Revenue, Customer Satisfaction, Review, Referral Generated.

## KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION
Automatically identify: Recurring Problems, Common Defects, Typical Costs, Typical Repair Time, FAQs, Best Practices, Lessons Learned.

## CONTENT ENGINE
Every completed project may generate: Blog Article, Facebook Post, LinkedIn Post, Educational Guide, Anonymous Case Study, Sales Material, Knowledge Base Entry, SEO Content.

## BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
Display: Most Common Problems, Most Profitable Services, Fastest Projects, Highest Customer Satisfaction, Highest Referral Rate, Highest ROI Services.

## COMPANY MEMORY
Every project increases PropManage intelligence. Future recommendations reuse knowledge from similar projects.

## FINAL DECLARATION
PropManage continuously builds the largest practical knowledge base about residential properties in Romania. Every completed project increases the company's long-term value.
