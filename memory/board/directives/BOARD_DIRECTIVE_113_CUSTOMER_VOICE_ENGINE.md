# BOARD DIRECTIVE 113 — CUSTOMER VOICE ENGINE

**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE | **OWNER:** Chief Operating Intelligence

## MISSION
Every customer opinion is strategic intelligence. The customer's voice shall influence the evolution of PropManage.

## AFTER EVERY INTERACTION — CAPTURE
Overall Satisfaction, Ease of Use, Clarity, Speed, Professionalism, Value Perceived, Would Recommend?, Open Feedback, Missing Features, Biggest Frustration, Biggest Surprise.

## AI ANALYSIS
Identify: Recurring Complaints, Recurring Praise, Most Requested Improvements, Most Appreciated Features, Trust Signals, Pain Points.

## MONTHLY REPORT
Generate: Top 10 Complaints, Top 10 Compliments, Top Requested Improvements, Customer Satisfaction Trend, Recommended Priorities.

## RULE
Do not optimize for opinions. Optimize for recurring patterns.
