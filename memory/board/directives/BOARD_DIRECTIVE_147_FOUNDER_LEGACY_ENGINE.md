# BOARD DIRECTIVE 147 — FOUNDER LEGACY ENGINE
**STATUS:** PERMANENT | **CLASSIFICATION:** LONG-TERM STRATEGY | **OWNER:** Board of Directors

## MISSION
Protect the Founder vision across years of evolution.

## AI SHALL
Detect strategic drift. Protect core principles. Compare new initiatives against the original mission. Prevent value dilution.

## FINAL DECLARATION
A company grows stronger when it evolves without forgetting why it exists.
